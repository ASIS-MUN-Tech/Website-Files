ASIS MUN is a student-led Model United Nations conference designed to turn curiosity into confident global leadership.

Think globally.
Lead together.

Organised by the ASIS MUN Secretariat, the conference encourages students to research global issues, represent national perspectives, negotiate with peers, and create practical solutions.

Through formal debate and collaborative problem-solving, delegates develop communication, leadership, teamwork, critical thinking, and responsible citizenship.
